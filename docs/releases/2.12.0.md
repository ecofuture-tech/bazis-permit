# bazis-permit 2.12.0

Requires bazis 2.23.

## Changes

- **The search reaches the fields of the card the field permissions show the user.**
  Bazis 2.23 searches the fields a route shows the user in the list or on the card (its
  LIST or RETRIEVE schema): a field the list leaves out to stay light (a long description)
  can be searched without adding it to every object of the list. `PermitRouteBase.query_fields(
  user=None, *, api_action=CrudApiAction.LIST, **kwargs)` now gives the fields of the
  schema of `api_action` (`query_schema_factory` of the core) that the field permissions
  of the user show in every object, by the same rule as for the list: shown by the
  permissions without a condition alone and with each conditional field group. So
  `search`, `$search` of `filter`, `<relation>__$search` and the `search_fields` of a route
  find by a field of its card where no field permission hides it, and never by a field
  hidden from the user in some object (on the list or on the card), on the route and
  through a relation of another route into the objects of the model (the default route of
  the model, restricted by its `restrict_queryset` as before).

  What stays:

  - The filter and the sorting reach only the fields of the LIST schema: a key of `filter`
    or `sort` by a field only the card shows is 400 `ERR_FILTER`, the answer for a field
    that does not exist.
  - A route that serves no card (no `action_retrieve` in its `actions`) searches its LIST
    fields only, also when the responses of its updates show more.
  - `query_fields(user)` without `api_action` gives the fields of LIST, as in 2.11.

## Compatibility

- **bazis 2.23 or newer** (`bazis>=2.23.0`): `query_fields` reads the factory of the action
  from `JsonapiRouteBase.query_schema_factory`, which older cores do not have. With
  bazis-permit 2.11 on bazis 2.23 nothing is exposed, the search of the routes of
  protected models stays within the LIST fields (the override of 2.11 gives them for
  RETRIEVE too); upgrade both to search the cards.
- A subclass of `PermitRouteBase` that overrides `query_fields` keeps `api_action`
  keyword-only, accepts `**kwargs` and narrows the fields of both actions the same way
  (call `super().query_fields(user, api_action=api_action, **kwargs)`): a field it leaves
  out of LIST only, while the card shows it, is searched.
- A route without `search_fields` searches the text fields of its LIST and RETRIEVE
  schemas (bazis 2.23), so its search may grow by the text fields of its card that the
  user sees: on a large list declare `search_fields` (or `[]`). The contract of bazis-front
  lists the search fields of a resource: generate it again.

## How to check

- A route whose LIST schema leaves out a text field its card shows
  (`fields = {CrudApiAction.LIST: SchemaFields(exclude={'description': None})}`, the
  sample route `entity.meeting_card`): `search=<a word of it>` finds the objects for a role
  that sees the field, `filter=description=...` and `sort=description` are 400.
- A role with `field.view.all.description.disable`, or a conditional one
  (`field.view.participants.description.disable`): the same search finds nothing.
- `manage.py bazis_doctor`: a declared search field that only the card shows is no longer
  `bazis.W007`.
