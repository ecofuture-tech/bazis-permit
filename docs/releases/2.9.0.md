# bazis-permit 2.9.0

## Features

- **Roles and permission groups declared in the code.** An application declares them in
  its `roles.py` module: `GROUPS` of `bazis.contrib.permit.declare.Group(slug, name,
  permissions)` and `ROLES` of `Role(slug, name, groups, for_anonymous=False)`. After
  `migrate` (the `post_migrate` signal, also sent by `flush`), once all the migrations of
  the project are applied, they are in the database, in one transaction:
  - the declared groups and roles are created or updated and marked with the new field
    `managed`;
  - the permissions of a managed group are exactly the declared ones, so a permission
    removed from the code is revoked; the managed groups of a declared role are exactly
    the declared ones, the groups the admin attached to it stay;
  - the roles and groups that are not declared are left as they are; a declared slug that
    exists unmarked (made by a data migration) is taken over, with a warning in the log;
  - applied again, nothing is written, and the cached permissions are invalidated only by
    a change;
  - the names are English msgids (`gettext_lazy`): `name_en` and `name_ru` get their
    translations from the catalogs of the project.

  A managed object no longer declared is kept (removal will be an explicit command).
  `apply_declarations(using, groups=None, roles=None, dry_run=False)` applies them from
  the code, or lists the changes.
- **Admin.** Managed groups and roles are read-only (a managed role still takes the groups
  of the admin); the declared ones are not deleted, also by the bulk delete.
- **Checks.** `permit.E004` (invalid slug, duplicate, a group of a role that is not
  declared) and `permit.E005` (a permission: its grammar, a `PermitModelMixin` model, a
  selector that is a relation to a `PermitSelectorMixin` model, an existing field, a known
  restriction) stop `migrate` and fail `bazis_doctor`; `permit.W004` lists the names
  without a translation. With a database (`manage.py check --database default`):
  `permit.W005`, the database differs from the declarations, and `permit.W006`, managed
  objects no longer declared. They are warnings because `migrate` runs the database checks
  before it applies the declarations; `bazis_doctor` does not run the database checks.

## Migrations

- `0008_managed`: the field `managed` of roles and permission groups (false for the
  existing ones).

## Upgrade

The data migrations of the roles of a product stay as history; to move the roles to
`roles.py`, declare the same slugs: the first `migrate` takes them over.
