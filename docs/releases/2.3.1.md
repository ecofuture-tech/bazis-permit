# bazis-permit 2.3.1

Requires Bazis 2.3.2.

## Security

- **Unlinking objects of a reverse relation was not checked.** With
  `BAZIS_PERMIT_RELATIONS_VIEW_CHECK` the objects a reverse relation links needed the
  change permission, but the objects it unlinks did not: an update that left them out of
  the list (or `PATCH`/`DELETE` of the relationships endpoints) changed their foreign key,
  or deleted them when the foreign key is required, without the permission. The unlinked
  objects need the change permission now.
- With Bazis 2.3.2 the `filter:` field permissions also apply to the objects the
  relationships endpoints unlink.

## Fixes

- `BAZIS_PERMISSION_CACHE_EXPIRE` is declared with `json_schema_extra` (an extra keyword
  argument of `Field` is deprecated in Pydantic 2 and emitted a warning).
