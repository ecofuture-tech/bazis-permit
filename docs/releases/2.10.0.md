# bazis-permit 2.10.0

## Changes

- `permit.W002` skips the routes that restrict their objects with their own
  `restrict_queryset` (an override of `RestrictedQsRouteMixin.restrict_queryset`, in the
  route or a base, as `FileUploadRouteSet` of bazis-uploadable and `BgRoute` of bazis-bg
  and their subclasses). Such a route no longer needs `permit_public = True`, which says
  that its data is public; remove it from these routes. The warning now reads that the
  route does not restrict its objects. The check sees the override, not that the
  `get_queryset` of the route applies it: the core does not apply it to the list and
  items of a route by itself.

## Docs

- The guide gives an example of field permissions with one field writable and the others
  read-only (`__all__.readonly` with `<field>.enable`), the precedence of the
  restrictions, and an implementation of `get_selector_for_user`.
